FitnessCaptain

Every session you have ever done, and what to do next.

A training log that remembers. Show it a gym, by photo or video, and it learns what is there. Meet a movement you have never done and it shows you pictures, a written how-to and a short demo video, from a library of 873 exercises. Lift, ride, stretch or flow. It logs all of it.

Free to start · works offline · syncs to every device you sign in on

Show it your gym
Take photos or film a walk round the room, whichever suits. It reads the equipment and remembers what that place has. Receipts and order screenshots work too, and they pin down exact models. You review everything before it saves, and video never leaves your phone.
When the gym is not your gym
You have a program you like, but the hotel has none of your usual kit. It tells you what will not work there before you start, and offers a movement you can actually do instead.
Someone may have scanned it already
Hotel gyms and clubs other people have shared, each one dated so you can see how current it is. Pull the equipment list into your gyms in one tap. Scan a place nobody has done yet and you save the next person the job.
Watch it, right here
Start and finish photos, a written how-to, and a short demo video that plays inside the app. No tab-hopping to YouTube mid-session.
873 exercises to draw on
Search a reference library of 873 exercises. Add one and it arrives complete: equipment, muscle group, written instructions, and pictures of the start and finish. A demo video is one tap away and plays inside the app.
Not just lifting
Barbells, the bike, a Pilates class, a stretch session. It asks for reps and weight when that is what the movement needs, and for minutes or distance when it is not.
Last time, set by set
Come back to a movement and every set you did last time is waiting, not just your best one. The numbers are already filled in, so logging is mostly tapping.
Knows when to add weight
One tap marks a set easy or a struggle. Easy, and next time it offers the heavier weight. A struggle, and it offers a lighter one. Most sets get no mark at all, which is the point.
Programs that advance
A rolling plan, not a calendar. Finish a day and the next one is waiting.
A workout built for today
Tell it how long you have, what you want to work and anything to avoid. It already knows the equipment where you are and what you trained this week, so it builds a session that fits and you start it in one tap. Ask for a change and it rebuilds.
Or a whole training block
Same idea, bigger scale. Give it your minutes a day, days a week, goals and gym, and it designs the full program. You edit whatever you disagree with before anything is saved.
Bring your history
Paste years of training from anywhere. It reads the sets, the fails and the machine settings.
Weekly read
What moved, what stalled, and one change for next week, grounded in your own numbers.

It never scores you, never guilts you, and never sells your training to anyone. If you stop for three weeks it will be exactly where you left it.

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads. Export everything or delete it outright, from Settings, whenever you like. Here is exactly what happens to your data.
