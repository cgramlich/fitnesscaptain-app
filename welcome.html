Whatever you actually do

Last time, in full

Knows when to add weight

Programs that advance
A rolling plan, not a calendar. Finish a day and the next one is waiting.
Build me a week
Tell it your minutes, days, goals and gym. It designs the week and you edit whatever you disagree with.
Bring your history
Paste years of training from anywhere. It reads the sets, the fails and the machine settings.
Weekly read
What moved, what stalled, and one change for next week, grounded in your own numbers.
Weight, honestly
Set a rate you are comfortable losing at. Go faster and it tells you, once.

It never scores you, never guilts you, and never sells your training to anyone. If you stop for three weeks it will be exactly where you left it.
