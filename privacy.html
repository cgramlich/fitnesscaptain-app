Privacy Policy

Effective 6 August 2026. Last updated 7 August 2026.

FitnessCaptain is a training log. It works because you tell it what you did, so almost everything in it is something you typed or photographed. This page says what happens to that.

The short version. Your training data is yours. It is not sold, not rented, not shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you. There are no analytics and no tracking pixels in this app. You can export everything or delete the account outright from Settings, and deletion is permanent.

Who runs this

FitnessCaptain is published by MilSpo Life LLC, a Texas limited liability company, as part of the Forever Apps portfolio. Questions about anything on this page go to support@fitnesscaptain.com.

What is collected

Because you gave it

  • Your email address, to create the account and sign you in. There is no password recovery without it.
  • Your training: workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, times, distances, routines, programs and any notes you write.
  • The places you train: gyms you add and the equipment you record or photograph in them.
  • Body measurements you choose to enter: bodyweight, waist, body fat, age. All optional. The app works without any of them.
  • Daily checklist entries, if you set one up.

Because the software needs it to run

  • An account identifier and sign-in session token.
  • Counts of AI requests and tokens used, per month, to enforce fair use limits. These are numbers only. The contents of AI requests are not stored or logged.
  • Ordinary server logs at our hosting providers, which include IP addresses, as with any website.

Location, only when you ask for it

There is one feature that uses where you are: the "gyms near me" and "hotels near me" search you can use when adding a gym. It is the only thing in the app that touches location, and nothing happens until you press the button.

  • Your device asks your permission first, and declining leaves everything else working. You can always search by name instead.
  • The coordinates are sent to our server, used to build one search request to Google, and then discarded. They are not stored in your account, not written to our logs, and never attached to a workout.
  • Nothing tracks you in the background. There is no location history, and the app cannot see where you are unless you are on that screen pressing that button.

Not collected

  • No background or continuous location, and no location history.
  • No contacts, no calendar, no microphone.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no analytics package of any kind.
  • No payment details. There is nothing to buy today, and if that changes a payment processor will handle card data directly.

Photos and video

The gym scan reads equipment out of pictures. Two things are worth knowing about how it handles them.

  • Photos are shrunk on your device before anything is sent. Resizing to roughly 1024 pixels re-encodes the image, which strips the embedded camera metadata, including any GPS coordinates your phone attached.
  • Video never leaves your device. When you film a walkthrough, the app pulls still frames out of the file locally and sends only those frames. The video itself is never uploaded.

Scan images are sent to Anthropic to be read, then discarded. They are not kept on our servers. What is saved is the equipment list you approve on the review screen.

Who else sees your data

Only these, only for the job named, and none of them for advertising.

Which data depends on the feature: planning a session sends your gym's equipment, your exercise library and your recent training; reading a gym photo sends the photo. What none of them get is a copy of your log to keep, and what nobody gets is your data in exchange for money. There is no data broker in this list because there is no data broker.

How long it is kept

Your data stays until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because a training log is worth more the longer it runs. If you delete your account, it goes immediately and permanently, including from backups on our providers' normal backup cycle.

What you can do

  • Export everything. Settings has an export that writes your whole log to a JSON file on your device. No request, no waiting.
  • Delete the account. Settings has a permanent delete that removes your training data and your sign-in. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • Correct anything. Every record in the app is editable by you at any time.
  • Ask a person. If you would rather someone did it for you, or you want to know exactly what is held, email support.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your data under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to access, correct, delete or port it, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. The two buttons above are how those rights are exercised here, and email covers anything they do not.

Security

Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth, so passwords are never seen by this app or stored on our servers. Traffic runs over HTTPS. Database access is restricted to the server: the database refuses direct reads and writes from browsers entirely, so a stolen client key gets an attacker nothing.

No system is perfect and it would be dishonest to promise otherwise. If something goes wrong that affects your data, you will be told what happened rather than left to read about it.

Children

FitnessCaptain is not intended for children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created one, email support and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that affects what happens to your data, the date at the top changes and the app tells you. Quiet edits to a privacy policy are a bad habit and this one will not make them.
