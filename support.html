Support

A real person reads this address.

Something broken, something confusing, or an idea for what it should do next: all of it is welcome at support@fitnesscaptain.com.

Email support

What to include when something is wrong. The screen you were on, what you expected, and what happened instead. If you can, open More, then Diagnostics and copy the log. It records what the app was doing without recording your training, and it usually answers the question on its own.

The usual suspects

The confirmation email never arrived

Check spam first, since a new sender takes a while to build a reputation. If it is not there, try signing up again with the same address. If it still does not arrive, email support from the address you are trying to use and the account can be confirmed by hand.

My data is not on my other device

Sync follows the account, not the device. Sign in with the same email address on both. If one device is showing older data, pull down on the workouts list to refresh, and check the status line at the top says all changes are saved rather than reporting an error.

It says it cannot reach the server

The app keeps working offline and saves everything locally, so nothing is lost. It will sync when the connection comes back. If it persists on a good connection, the backend or one of its providers may be having an outage. Email support and it will be looked at.

An AI feature stopped working

Each account has a monthly limit on AI requests, set high enough that ordinary use does not reach it. If you have hit it, the AI features pause until the next month while everything else keeps working. Any other AI error is worth reporting.

The gym scan missed equipment

Filming a walkthrough usually beats separate photos, because it samples frames across the whole clip. Walk slowly and sweep the room once. Receipts and order screenshots work too and are better than photos at pinning down exact models. Whatever it reads, you get a review screen before anything saves, so miscounts are fixable there.

A video demo will not play

Demos are YouTube embeds, so they need a connection and they follow YouTube's own availability. If a particular movement has a poor demo, the search link next to it will find a better one.

Your data, on demand

  • Export it from Settings. You get your entire log as a JSON file, immediately, no request needed.
  • Delete it from Settings. Permanent, and it takes the account with it. Export first if you want a copy.
  • If you would rather a person did either of those, or you want to know exactly what is held about you, just ask.

The Privacy Policy sets out the whole picture.

Feature requests

Most of what is in this app exists because somebody said it was annoying. If something is getting in your way, that is worth an email even if you cannot say exactly what the fix should be.

How fast to expect a reply

This is a small independent product, so support is a person and not a department. Expect a few days rather than a few minutes. Anything involving lost data or a broken account goes to the front of the queue.
